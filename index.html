The Craft

Gifts designed to be remembered.

Mint & Co. is a Lahore-based gift company crafting luxury keepsake boxes for life's most meaningful moments — announcements, weddings, new arrivals, Hajj & Umrah, and every special occasion in between.

Every piece is hand-finished with premium materials, delicate detailing and thoughtful curation, then made completely your own. Beautiful to open, and built to be kept long after the gift inside.
